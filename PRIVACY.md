# LiveAstroStudio Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 4, 2026**

LiveAstroStudio is a macOS application for capturing, stacking, displaying, and
processing astronomical images. The app is designed to process your images on
your Mac.

## Information the app handles

- Image files, calibration files, session notes, and settings that you choose
  to open or save are read and written locally on your Mac.
- Camera, telescope, and observatory data is used locally to operate a session
  when you connect compatible equipment.
- If you explicitly configure an output or streaming service, the app sends the
  images or status information required for that service. Those transfers are
  controlled by your configuration and governed by the service's own privacy
  policy.

## Information the app does not collect

LiveAstroStudio does not include advertising, third-party analytics, tracking
pixels, or a remote user account. It does not sell personal information or
share your files for advertising or profiling.

The app may create local logs, session summaries, and support bundles when you
use its diagnostics features. These remain on your Mac unless you choose to
send them to someone for support.

## Your choices

You control which folders, devices, and optional services the app can access.
You can remove locally stored sessions, logs, and support bundles using the
normal macOS file tools.

## Contact

For privacy questions or support, please open an issue at
<https://github.com/paulydavis/liveastro-studio/issues>.

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. The effective
date at the top of this page identifies the current version.
